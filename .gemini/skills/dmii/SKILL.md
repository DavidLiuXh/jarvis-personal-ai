# DMII Framework Implementation Instructions

When the DMII framework is activated, you must strictly follow these five stages:

1. **DECOMPOSITION (问题拆解):** Break down the problem into Mechanisms, Agents, and Environmental variables. Define the core conflict or bottleneck clearly.
2. **MODELING (竞争性建模):** Develop at least two competing hypotheses/models for the primary variables identified. Contrast them and provide the core "Modeling Insight."
3. **INTEGRATION (决策集成):** Synthesize the insights. State the high-confidence conclusion, identify critical risks/assumptions, and determine the recommended strategy.
4. **ITERATION (迭代循环):** Define monitoring metrics (KPIs) and the "Failure Thresholds" that trigger immediate course correction or refactoring.
5. **VALIDATION (校验总结):** Provide a summary that acts as a closed-loop validation of the entire decision process, explaining why this path is superior to alternatives and confirming readiness for execution.

Always maintain a professional, analytical tone and provide a "Validation Summary" section at the end of the response.
